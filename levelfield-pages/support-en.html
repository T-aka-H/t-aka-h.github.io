Frequently asked

How do I play?

Tap a square and it rises by one, while the four squares beside it drop by one. Heights wrap at three: a square pushed past the top falls all the way to zero. Make every square the same height and the plate is done — any height will do. How to play is always available from the menu.

A block is hiding a square and I cannot tap it

Press Turn, at the bottom right, to rotate the sheet by ninety degrees. Hold it and the sheet keeps turning, stopping wherever you let go. A square that no angle can recover is drawn as a red dashed outline, and pressing that outline moves the square it belongs to.

What is par?

The fewest taps the plate can be solved in. As in golf, coming in under par is marked with a ring and going over with a box. You can finish a plate however many taps it takes.

What does Pro include?

Stages 21 onward, out of 108. It is a one-off purchase, not a subscription. There is no advertising in the App, free or paid. The daily puzzle is free to everyone.

Will my purchase carry over to a new device?

Yes, on the same Apple Account — press Restore Purchases on the Pro screen. Note that your solved records are stored only on the device, so those do not carry over.

There is no sound

The App honours the silent switch, deliberately, so it never makes a noise you did not expect in public. If sound is still missing with the switch off, check that Sound is turned on in the menu.

The leaderboards are empty

They need Game Center. Sign in from Settings → Game Center on your device. The whole game is playable without it.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the App removes your records and progress from the device. A Pro purchase stays on your Apple Account, so Restore Purchases brings it back after reinstalling.
