Blocks of different heights stand on a sheet. Make every square the same height and the plate is done. Any height will do.

Tap a square and it rises by one, while the four squares beside it drop by one. Heights wrap at three. That is the whole rule; everything after it is deciding where to press, and in what order.

What is in it

  • 108 plates, growing from 2×2 to 5×5
  • A par for every plate, kept on a scorecard read like golf
  • A new puzzle each day
  • Turn the sheet to see behind a block
  • Solved plates are kept, with the board and the moves you made

No advertising. Nothing to sign up for. It honours the silent switch.
