BattingLab

Swing, launch, measure — exit velocity, launch angle and distance on every ball.

Support Privacy

BattingLab is a home run derby for iPhone. Your timing and where you meet the ball decide the swing, and every batted ball reports its exit velocity, launch angle and distance. The ball flies through a 3D ballpark, so you can watch whether it clears the fence.

Three modes

  • 10 BALLS — how many home runs in ten pitches. The last ball is pink, and a home run adds one more pitch.
  • 60 SECONDS — how many you can hit in a minute. Long home runs add time.
  • PRACTICE — a mode for warming up. Not eligible for the leaderboards.

Leaderboards

Sign in to Game Center to join the leaderboards for 10 balls, 60 seconds and longest home run (rounds played without assist). You can play every mode without signing in.

BattingLab Pro

A one-time in-app purchase. It unlocks the ballpark presets and the ballpark editor, the "what if this round had been played in another park" recalculation, detailed practice settings (pitch type, speed and location), session history with trend graphs, spray charts and CSV export, per-ballpark leaderboards, and the shareable summary card. It is not a subscription.

No account is required and there are no ads. Your records stay on your device.
