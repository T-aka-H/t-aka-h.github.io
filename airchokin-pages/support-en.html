Frequently asked

Does this actually save money?

No. Air Chokin records the discount printed on a receipt as a saving you keep track of. No money moves, nothing is deposited anywhere, and there is no bank or card connection.

The app is Japanese only

It reads Japanese receipt layouts, and the words it looks for (小計 / 合計 / お値引) are specific to them. The interface is Japanese for the same reason.

What if an amount cannot be read?

Drag a box around the amount on the photograph and the app reads it again. If that still fails you can type it. The app never requires recognition to succeed — you can always reach the right figure.

What if the total or the date is read wrongly?

On the confirmation screen, tap the row you meant and choose 「これを合計にする」 (use this as the total). The date can be tapped and set. After recording, open the receipt from Spending or Receipts and choose 「読み取りを修正」 to read it again.

Moving to a new phone

Settings can export your figures to a file and import them again. Importing only adds — it never deletes what is already on the device. Photographs are not in the export; they can be saved to the Photos app from the Receipts tab.

Is it free?

Photographing receipts, reading the discount, enclosing and typing amounts by hand, and exporting your data are all free. Air Chokin Pro (a one-time purchase, ¥200 in Japan) adds receipt search, exporting receipt photographs, multiple goals, and changing the accent colour later. It is not a subscription. Previous purchases can be restored from Settings.

Where is my data kept?

On your device only. Reading and totalling happen on the device and the app makes no network requests.
