Air Chokin — Privacy Policy

HomeSupport

Last updated: 3 August 2026

Air Chokin records the discounts printed on your receipts as a virtual saving. No money is moved and nothing is deposited into any bank account. There is no connection to any financial institution and no transfer function.

This policy explains how information is handled in Air Chokin (the app).

1. Information collected

The app collects and stores no personally identifying information. No account is required. It contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

The following is stored on your device only:

  • Photographs of receipts you take or import
  • Text and figures read from them (shop, subtotal, discount, total, item lines)
  • Settings such as your goal, your monthly ceiling and your conversion unit

2. On-device processing

Text recognition (OCR) and every figure the app derives are computed on your device. Receipt images and recognised text are never sent to the developer or to any third party.

3. Device permissions

  • Camera — to photograph a receipt. If you decline, receipts can still be imported from Photos or entered by hand.
  • Photos (add only) — used only when you choose to save a receipt image to the Photos app. The app never reads your photo library.

Choosing a receipt from Photos uses the system picker, so full photo-library access is not required.

4. Network

The app makes no network requests. Recording, reading and totalling all happen on the device, and no data leaves it.

5. Third-party services

No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services are used.

6. Export and deletion

Settings lets you export your recorded figures to a file and import them again. Where you keep that file is up to you.

Receipt images are never deleted automatically. If you want to remove them you choose a period yourself in Settings. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.

7. In-app purchase (Air Chokin Pro)

The app offers a one-time in-app purchase, Air Chokin Pro, which unlocks some features. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit and Apple's privacy policy applies. The developer never receives payment details such as card numbers — only the fact that a purchase exists.

8. Children's privacy

The app is rated 4+. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 — it collects none at all and sends nothing anywhere.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date at the top is revised.

10. Contact

Please use the contact method shown on the app's App Store page.
