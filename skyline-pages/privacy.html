Skyline Widget — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-01 (v1.0.0)

日本語 English Français Español Italiano

Skyline Widget ("the App") is developed and operated by an independent developer. This Privacy Policy describes how the App handles user information.

Summary

  • The App collects no personal data and assigns you no identifier of any kind — no device ID, no advertising ID, no UUID, no session token.
  • The App contains no third-party analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs. It sends anonymous counts to the developer's own server — see "Anonymous Usage Statistics".
  • Your exact location never leaves your device. Only a rounded, coarse area is included in the anonymous statistics.
  • The App displays no advertisements.
  • Your settings (chosen place, scenery, frame colour) are stored locally on your device.

Data Collection

The App collects no personal data. Your settings — the place you picked, the scenery, the window frame colour, the twinkle setting — are stored on your device using iOS standard storage (UserDefaults, in an App Group shared with the widget) and never leave it in that form.

Anonymous Usage Statistics

To decide which parts of the App are worth maintaining, it sends anonymous counts to a server operated by the developer (collect.appline.app, on Cloudflare). Each message contains exactly four fields: the app name, an event name from a fixed list, the app version, and — for some events — one detail value drawn from a fixed vocabulary.

It is therefore not technically possible to reconstruct one person's activity, to count distinct users, or to follow anyone over time. The server independently enforces the fixed list: anything outside it — including free text — is discarded and never stored.

Location

The App can calculate sunrise and sunset times for where you are. This uses Apple's location services only if you tap "Use current location" and grant permission. The App requests reduced accuracy — it never asks for your precise position.

Your coordinates stay on your device. What is included in the anonymous statistics is a rounded grid cell, never a raw coordinate:

  • A place you searched for or picked from the list: rounded to about 5 km (a cell of roughly 25 km²). This describes the view you chose, not where you are.
  • A place obtained from "Use current location": rounded to about 50 km. This is deliberately coarser, because in that case the coordinate does describe roughly where you are.

The server rejects any value that has not been rounded, so a raw coordinate cannot be stored even by mistake. No street, address, or precise position is ever transmitted.

You can use the App fully without granting location access — simply search for a city instead.

Choosing a Place

You choose a place by tapping a map. Map tiles come from Apple Maps, and the tapped coordinate is sent to Apple's geocoding service to obtain a place name and time zone. If you search by name, the text you type is also sent to Apple so it can be resolved into coordinates and a time zone. This is Apple's own service, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer does not receive your search text, and it is never included in the anonymous statistics.

Notifications

If you turn on the sunset reminder, notifications are scheduled entirely on your device. No push server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere.

The Widget

The widget makes no network requests at all. Everything it draws — the sky colour, the sun and moon position, the stars — is computed on the device from the date and the place you chose.

Third-Party Services

The App integrates no third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or data brokers. It uses only Apple's own frameworks (SwiftUI, WidgetKit, CoreLocation, UserNotifications). The only non-Apple destination is the developer's own endpoint collect.appline.app, described above; it is operated by the developer, not a third party, and involves no analytics SDK.

Tracking

The App does not track you. It does not link data to you or your device, does not combine data with information from other companies, and does not share data with data brokers. It does not present the App Tracking Transparency prompt because it has nothing to ask for.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages (Apple Age Rating 4+). It does not knowingly collect data from children. Since it collects no personal data and assigns no identifier, this holds for every user equally.

Changes to this Policy

If we change this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be communicated through the App's release notes.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us here.

位置情報

本アプリは、現在地の日の出・日の入り時刻を計算できます。Apple の位置情報サービスを使うのは、「現在地を使う」を押して許可した場合のみです。取得は低精度（reduced accuracy）で要求しており、精密な位置を求めることはありません。

座標は端末内に留まります。匿名の統計に含まれるのは丸めた升目だけで、生の座標が送られることはありません。

  • 検索または一覧から選んだ場所: 約5kmに丸めます（およそ25km²の区画）。これは「選んだ眺め」であって、あなたの居場所ではありません。

サーバーは丸められていない値を受け付けないため、誤って生の座標が保存されることもありません。番地・住所・精密な位置が送信されることは一切ありません。

位置情報を許可しなくても、都市を検索して選べば本アプリのすべての機能を利用できます。

場所の選択

場所は地図を押して選びます。地図は Apple マップから配信され、押された座標は地名と時間帯を得るため Apple のジオコーディングサービスへ送信されます。名前で検索した場合、入力された文字列も Apple へ送信されます。これは Apple 自身のサービスであり、Apple のプライバシーポリシーに従います。開発者が検索文字列を受け取ることはなく、匿名の統計に含まれることもありません。

通知

日の入りの通知を有効にした場合、通知はすべて端末内で予約されます。プッシュ通知サーバーは介在せず、どこにも何も送信されません。

ウィジェットについて

ウィジェットはネットワーク通信を一切行いません。空の色も、太陽・月の位置も、星も、すべて日付と選ばれた場所から端末上で計算しています。

本アプリはサードパーティの SDK・分析サービス・広告ネットワーク・データブローカーを一切使用していません。使用しているのは Apple 自身のフレームワーク（SwiftUI、WidgetKit、CoreLocation、UserNotifications）のみです。Apple 以外の唯一の送信先は、上記の匿名カウントを送る開発者自身のエンドポイント collect.appline.app です。第三者ではなく開発者が運用しており、分析 SDK は介在しません。
