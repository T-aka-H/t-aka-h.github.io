Journeyline is an iOS / iPadOS / macOS app provided by Takanori Hamuro ("we"). This policy describes how information is handled in the app.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect or store personally identifiable information. No account sign-up is required.

The following stays on your device (and optionally in your iCloud account):

  • Pins (place names, notes, visit status, planned dates)
  • Diary entries and attached photos
  • Custom lists
  • In-app preferences

2. Device permissions

  • Location (when in use) — current location, reverse geocoding, and reading GPS from photos. No background tracking.
  • Photo library — attaching photos to diary entries, importing places from photos, and reading Google Takeout export files you choose.
  • Motion — optional decorative effect on the home screen when you tilt the device. Not used for health or activity tracking.

3. iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, pins, diary entries, and photos may sync across your devices via Apple iCloud under Apple's privacy policy. We do not operate a backend server for your content.

4. Third-party services

  • Apple MapKit — map display and place search
  • Google Maps — only when you choose to open directions or view a place in Google Maps

No advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or social login.

5. Share Extension

Links shared from Safari or Google Maps are stored temporarily in an App Group inbox on your device until you import them as a pin. Nothing is sent to our servers.

6. Children's privacy

The app is rated 4+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
