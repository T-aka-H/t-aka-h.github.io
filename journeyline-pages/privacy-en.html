This policy describes how information is handled in Journeyline (the "App").

1. Information we collect

We do not collect or store personally identifiable information. No account sign-up is required.

The following stays on your device (and optionally in your iCloud account), and is never sent to our servers:

  • Pins (place names, notes, visit status, planned dates)
  • Diary entries and attached photos
  • Custom lists
  • In-app preferences

The only thing that leaves your device is the anonymous usage statistics (identity-free counts) described in Section 2.

2. Anonymous Usage Statistics

To make the App better, it sends anonymous usage counts to a server operated by the developer (collect.appline.app, on Cloudflare). Three kinds of things are counted, all as totals (aggregates) only:

  • How many times the app is launched (a rough measure of how much it is used)
  • Which features are used (e.g. a pin was saved, a place was added from photos, a place was marked visited, a diary entry was created, the guided tour was opened — so we can find features that are unused and improve them)
  • Which screen tends to follow which (e.g. "map → add menu"), as total transition counts from a fixed screen list — never an individual's sequence

What is sent (only this): a fixed, predefined event or screen name plus the app version number. Nothing else.

What is never sent: place names, coordinates, addresses, photos, or diary text; no name, email, account information, device identifier (IDFA / IDFV), advertising ID, or current location. We count only that a pin was saved — never where it was.

No individual can be identified or tracked: the App assigns no persistent identifier to a device or user (no device ID, no session ID). All that is stored is "how many times, in total, something happened" — there is no key to join these counts on, which makes it structurally impossible to identify a specific user, follow the same person over time, or build a profile. Screen transitions are stored only as "A→B happened N times in total"; no individual route can be reconstructed.

Where it goes: only to the developer's own server (on Cloudflare). It is never shared with any third party such as Google, Firebase, or Meta. Your IP address is not logged, stored, or used, and is never associated with the anonymous counts above.

3. Device permissions

  • Location (when in use) — current location, reverse geocoding, and reading GPS from photos. No background tracking.
  • Photo library — attaching photos to diary entries, importing places from photos, and reading Google Takeout export files you choose.
  • Motion — optional decorative effect on the home screen when you tilt the device. Not used for health or activity tracking.

4. iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, pins, diary entries, and photos may sync across your devices via Apple iCloud under Apple's privacy policy. We do not operate a backend server for your content.

5. Third-party services & tracking

  • Apple MapKit — map display and place search
  • Google Maps — only when you choose to open directions or view a place in Google Maps

No advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics SDKs (Google Analytics, Firebase, Meta, etc.), and no social login. The anonymous counts in Section 2 are sent only to the developer's own server, are not linked to your identity, and are not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. They therefore do not constitute tracking under App Tracking Transparency (ATT), and no ATT prompt is issued.

6. Share Extension

Links shared from Safari or Google Maps are stored temporarily in an App Group inbox on your device until you import them as a pin. The link and place content are never sent to our servers (per Section 2, we only count that an import happened).

7. Children's privacy

The app is rated 4+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The anonymous usage statistics in Section 2 are identifier-free totals only and contain nothing that identifies any person, including a child.
