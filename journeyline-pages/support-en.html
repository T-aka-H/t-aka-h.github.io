FAQ

Import from Google Maps

Open a place in Google Maps, tap Share → choose Journeyline, or copy the link and use Add from link on the map screen.

Add a place from a photo

On the map, tap + → Add from photos and pick a photo with location metadata. Photos without GPS are skipped.

Where is my data stored?

Pins, diary entries, and photos stay on your device. If you use iCloud, they sync across your devices signed in with the same Apple ID. We do not operate a backend server for your content.

Location permission

Location is used only while you use the app—for the current-location button and reverse geocoding. We do not track your location in the background.

iPad and Mac

The same app runs on iPad and Apple Silicon Mac (Designed for iPad).
