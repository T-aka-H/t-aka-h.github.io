FAQ

Import from Google Maps

Open a place in Google Maps, tap Share → choose Add to Journeyline, or copy the link and use Add from link on the map screen.

Add a place from a photo

On the map, tap + → Add from photos and pick a photo with location metadata. Photos without GPS are skipped.

Where is my data stored?

Pins, diary entries, and photos stay on your device. If you use iCloud, they sync across your devices signed in with the same Apple ID. We do not operate a backend server for your content.

Location permission

Location is used while you use the app—for the current-location button and reverse geocoding. Only if you turn on Nearby Reminders (Journeyline Pro), the app asks for "Always" access so it can notify you when it is closed. Your location is used on your device only and is never sent anywhere.

What is Journeyline Pro? (price and restore)

From version 1.2, Journeyline Pro is available as a one-time purchase. There is no subscription. The price is shown in your local currency on the purchase screen (Settings → Journeyline Pro).

Pro unlocks three features: the trip summary image, Nearby Reminders, and Home Screen widgets. The map, pins, lists, diary, photos and iCloud sync are free without it.

After changing devices or reinstalling, use Settings → Restore Purchases to get your purchase back on the same Apple Account.

I was near a place but got no reminder

Check that the Nearby Reminders switch under Settings → Reminders is on, and that in iPhone Settings, notifications are allowed for Journeyline and location is set to "Always". With "While Using", reminders arrive only while the app is open. The same place is not announced again right away.

iPad and Mac

The same app runs on iPad and Apple Silicon Mac (Designed for iPad).
