Journeyline

Plan places. Visit them. Remember.

Support Privacy

Journeyline is a place journal on the map: save spots as Want to go, Planned, or Visited, add diary notes and photos, track prefectures and countries you've been to, and share a record poster to social media.

Search for places, import shared links from Google Maps, add pins from photo locations, organize custom lists, and optionally sync with iCloud. No ads or account sign-up required.
