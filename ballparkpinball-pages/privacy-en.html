Ballpark Pinball — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled in Ballpark Pinball and on this website.

1. Information collected

The app does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or device identifiers. It includes no advertising or tracking SDKs and no third-party analytics SDKs. World rankings use Apple’s Game Center.

Anonymous usage counts: to improve the app, it sends only the number of times certain things happen to a developer-operated server (collect.appline.app): the app was opened; a game was started on a built-in ballpark, an imported ballpark, or in practice; a game on a built-in ballpark was finished; a ballpark was imported; the world rankings were opened; and the purchase screen was opened, the purchase button was tapped, or a purchase was completed or restored. Only three items are sent: the app name, the kind of event, and the app version. Ballpark names, scores, the contents of imported ballparks, device or account identifiers, and location are never sent. Because no per-user identifier exists, events cannot be linked to a person or to each other; only totals are counted. Nothing is shared with third parties.

2. On-device storage and processing

Your best scores and stars for each ballpark, settings such as the camera, imported ballpark data, and the ballpark pictures shown in the menu are stored on your device. Imported ballparks (StadiumLab .stadiumlab / .blpark files) are read on your device only so you can play them, and are never sent to a developer server. The app does not use the camera, microphone, or location.

3. Game Center and rankings

When you sign in to Game Center, the app uses your player identifier, display name, scores and ranks. When you finish a game on one of the three built-in ballparks, that score and the total of your three built-in bests are submitted to Game Center. World rankings show Game Center display names, scores and ranks. Scores on imported ballparks are not submitted. Every game is playable without signing in.

4. Information handled by Apple

Apple handles Game Center information under its services and privacy policies. You can review profile and activity sharing in your device’s Game Center settings.

Game Center & Privacy / Apple Privacy Policy

Payments for the in-app purchase (Ballpark Pinball Pro) are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app never receives your name, payment method, card number, or other payment details. It only checks on your device, through Apple’s StoreKit, whether the purchase has been made.

5. Deleting stored data

Deleting the app removes the records and imported ballparks stored on your device. Offloading the app may keep its data. Scores already submitted to Game Center are managed separately by Apple.

6. Support correspondence

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, anything you include, and your message. We use them only as needed to answer you and investigate problems.

7. Children’s privacy

The app has no chat, photo sharing, or advertising. Parents can restrict Game Center and in-app purchases with Apple’s Screen Time.

8. This website

This site is served by GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics or advertising cookies. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See GitHub Pages data collection.

9. Changes to this policy

If features or how information is handled change, this page and its date will be updated.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact us by email. Help is also on the support page.
