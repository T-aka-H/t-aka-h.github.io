For problems or requests, contact us by email. Including your device model, iOS version and what happened helps us look into it.

Frequently asked questions

Getting started

Choose a ballpark and tap Play. Hold either Swing button to pitch, and lift your finger on green to swing. The yellow ring is your next target. Hit all three targets to load the bases, then go for a grand slam.

What are the three views?

The top two follow the ball and show the whole ballpark; the bottom one looks down on the field. Use the camera button at the lower right to switch to a single view or another angle.

How do I play my own ballpark?

Export a ballpark from StadiumLab (a separate app) as .stadiumlab or as a .blpark with ballpark data, then open it from My ballparks on the home screen. You can also open the file from the Files app or AirDrop. Importing ballparks is a Ballpark Pinball Pro feature. The three built-in ballparks and world rankings are free.

How do I restore my purchase?

After changing devices or reinstalling, open the purchase screen from My ballparks and tap Restore Purchase. Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. For refunds, use Apple’s Report a Problem (reportaproblem.apple.com).

How do I get on the world rankings?

Sign in to Game Center. When you finish a game on one of the three built-in ballparks, that score and the total of your three bests are submitted. Tap the trophy at the top of the home screen to see your rank inside the app. Imported ballparks keep personal bests on your device.

Which devices and languages are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS / iPadOS 17 or later. The app is available in English and Japanese, following your device language.
