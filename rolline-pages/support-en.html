Questions

Does it cost anything?

The ten practice courses and the twenty of the Open League are free, all of them. Drills gives you one free course a day to try. Rolline Pro is a one-time purchase that opens the Pro League and the Workshop and removes the limit on drills. It is not a subscription. If you need it again on another device, use Restore purchase on the Pro screen.

The ball never reaches the goal

A climb costs speed and a drop returns some of it, but what you get back from coming down does not pay for going up again — height is spent, not borrowed. Corners cost speed too, so a long or twisty route runs out sooner. Where it stopped is marked on the sheet; try a shorter line, or one that climbs less.

What is the total?

The number of pieces you used. A league ranks by that total across the whole set, fewest first. The total only exists once every course in the set has been finished — a total over nineteen courses and a total over twenty are not comparable, so partial ones are not counted.

How do I give a course to somebody?

Build one in the Workshop and roll it home; Give then becomes available. Seven characters travel, and whoever receives them opens the course from Open a code. What arrives is the problem — the answer does not travel with it. Once they have solved it themselves, they can look at how you did it.

The leaderboard is empty

Leaderboards use Game Center. Check that you are signed in under Settings → Game Center. Even signed out, your own total and its breakdown are shown in the app.
