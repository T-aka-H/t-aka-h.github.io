Sizesnap Lab

An iPhone / iPad app that measures a garment laid flat using AR, keeps the numbers in your closet, and lets you compare them against whatever you are about to buy.

Support Privacy Policy Terms

What it does

  • Measure in AR — lay the garment flat and tap the two ends. No tape measure needed.
  • Kept with the photo — shoulder width, body length, chest and more, recorded in centimeters alongside the photo you measured.
  • Compare with what fits you — register a garment that already fits well, and see the difference part by part.
  • Closet — revisit saved garments as a list or a gallery.
  • Listing text — turn the measurements into a size description you can copy.

What you can measure

Tops, bottoms, outerwear and dresses — plus anything else you can lay flat, such as a bag, a hat or a cushion. Choosing your own measurement points ("custom measurements") is a Pro feature.

For questions, please use the Sizesnap Lab product page on the App Store.
