Send one link and a park, a campus, or a mall becomes the field. The seeker gets a radar of direction and distance. The hider gets nothing but a warning that someone is closing in.

Different roles see different worlds

Whoever creates the link is the hider; whoever opens it is the seeker. After a 60-second hiding countdown the radar wakes up. The seeker gets a full instrument panel — bearing, distance, closing speed, even floor difference. The hider gets a five-level threat meter and nothing else.

The closer you get, the sharper it reads

Far out, GPS gives a rough bearing. Closer in, Bluetooth picks them up. Within a few metres, ultra-wideband locks on to a tenth of a metre. The barometer detects floor differences too, so multi-storey buildings make the game deeper.

Escape, or be found

Choose a limit of 5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes. Find the hider in time and the seeker wins; survive and the hider does. Results are kept in your records on the device — never the place you played.

Nothing is left behind

No account. Location is shared only while the app is open, and always mutually. When the game ends the session is deleted, and every link expires automatically after three hours.
