Frequently asked questions

What does Read-OK do?

It lets you read aloud public-domain Japanese literature and measure expressive reading (presence, melody, pacing, dynamics, finish) on your device. Literature to savor; science to measure.

How much does it cost?

Downloading the app and measuring curated literature — including previous-self comparison and rankings — is free. No ads. No account required.

Read-OK Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks pasting your own text to measure. The price is shown on the App Store purchase screen. It is not a subscription. Restore from the Pro screen or Settings → Restore purchases.

How do I take a first measurement?

On Home, pick a classic passage (or choose from Recently popular) → start recording on the reading screen → after analysis you'll see scores and readings. Allow the microphone when prompted (audio is not sent off your device).

Can I use English?

Yes. On iPhone go to Settings → Read-OK → Language and choose English. In English UI the passage is horizontal, with furigana and romaji under annotated words.

What are the rankings?

You can compare curated-literature scores across six boards: presence, melody, pacing, dynamics, finish, and overall. Sign into Game Center to join world rankings. Custom-passage (Pro) scores never enter rankings.

Where is my data stored?

Scores, settings, and custom-passage history stay on your device. Audio analysis also runs on device. Recordings are deleted after analysis and are never sent to the developer's servers.

Sometimes it says the measurement can't be scored

If noise is high or the voice is too quiet / too close for a reliable reading, the app skips scoring and shows why. Try a quieter place and hold the phone a little farther away.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked

Try Restore purchases on the Pro screen or in Settings. If it still fails, contact us via the App Store page.
