Support Privacy

Read-OK scores your practice when you read out loud. Paste the script for a speech or a presentation, read it aloud, and see your presence, melody, pacing, dynamics and finish — measured on your device.

For practice, it also includes 13 famous English passages in the public domain (Lincoln, Shakespeare, Dickens and more) and 40 Japanese classics with furigana and romaji. Audio is deleted after on-device analysis and is never uploaded.

Measure one script of about two minutes for free. Read-OK Pro, a one-time purchase, keeps as many scripts as you like and measures longer ones, up to about five minutes. The classics stay free. No ads. No account.
