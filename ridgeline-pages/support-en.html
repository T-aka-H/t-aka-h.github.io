FAQ

Nothing is drawn

Elevation data is fetched over the internet — please check your connection. Areas over open sea or without elevation data may show no lines.

The coastline view is blank

COAST mode draws only where the sea meets land. Inland spots show “No coastline in this area.” Widen the range or pick a coastal spot.

Even low hills look tall

With SCALE set to AUTO, the highest point in the range always fills the frame. Switch SCALE to REAL (relative to Everest) to draw true-to-height.

Can I draw places outside Japan?

Yes. Search or pick anywhere on the map. Japan uses GSI data; elsewhere uses Mapzen Terrain Tiles.

Where is my data stored?

All your pieces are stored only on your device. No account, no personal data collection.
