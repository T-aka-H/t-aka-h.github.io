Meettap — Privacy Policy

Home Support Privacy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This policy explains how Meettap handles information. The app shares the minimum needed to meet up, only with the person you are meeting, and only while you are meeting.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect or store personally identifying information. There is no account to create, and we never register or collect your name, email address, phone number, or social accounts. No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs are included.

2. Information shared during a meet-up

While a meet-up session is active, the following is shared only with the person you are meeting.

  • Approximate location: not your exact coordinates, but a position rounded to a roughly 100-meter grid, used only for direction and rough distance
  • Device heading: so the arrow points the right way
  • A single-use ranging token: an identifier that lets the two devices measure distance directly. It is created fresh for each session and cannot be used for anything else

Sharing is always mutual. There is no state in which only one side can see the other. Sharing happens only while the app is open, and there is no background location access or tracking.

3. Processed on device only

Precise close-range distance and direction are computed directly between the two devices and never sent to a server. The camera is used solely to improve directional accuracy, and no video is stored or transmitted. Bluetooth is used only to sense proximity.

4. Handling on our server

The information in section 2 passes through our server (meettap.appline.app, on Cloudflare) to make the meet-up work.

  • Only the rounded position, heading, single-use token, and session state are stored — nothing that identifies a person
  • It is deleted immediately when you meet, or when either side ends the session
  • If neither happens, it disappears automatically three hours after the link was created
  • We do not record access logs or IP addresses

No meet-up history remains on the server or on your device.

5. Device permissions

  • Location (while in use): to show distance and direction to the other person
  • Nearby device interaction: to measure precise distance and direction
  • Bluetooth: to sense proximity
  • Camera: to improve directional accuracy (no video is stored or sent)

All are used only while the meet-up screen is open.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will be revised.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, please use the contact method on the app's App Store page. If you are using a beta build, you can also use TestFlight's feedback feature.
