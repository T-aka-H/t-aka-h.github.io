Frequently asked questions

Does the other person need Meettap installed?

After public release, no. Opening the link launches a lightweight App Clip that goes straight to the meet-up screen. During the beta period, both sides should install the app from TestFlight.

How much of my location can the other person see?

While you are far apart, only a position rounded to a roughly 100-meter grid is shared, so your exact address is never revealed. Once you are close, precise distance and direction are computed directly between the two devices and are never sent to a server.

Can one side search for the other without being seen?

No. Location sharing is always mutual. Whenever you can see the other person, they can see you.

How long does a link stay valid?

It stops working once you meet, once either side ends the session, or three hours after it was created. An expired link reveals nothing.

Is any data kept after we meet?

Nothing is kept on the server — the session is deleted the moment you meet. On your device, a record is saved only on the occasions you tap "Save this meet-up" on the meet-up screen. Nothing is saved by default, and the location itself is never recorded.

What does a saved meet-up contain?

The date and time, the farthest distance seen while tracking, the distance when you met, and how long it took. The app has no way of knowing who the other person is, so any name is one you type yourself. Records stay on this device only and are never sent to our server or to iCloud. You can delete them individually or all at once from the list.

What can I do on Apple Watch?

Start a meet-up on your iPhone and the distance and arrow appear on your wrist. The Watch mirrors what the iPhone shows, so it does no location gathering or networking of its own and every generation of Apple Watch works. Installing the iPhone app installs the Watch app too. Keep the iPhone app open during a meet-up (it is fine in your pocket).

The arrow or distance is not showing

Check that location access is allowed (Settings → Meettap → Location → While Using the App), and keep the app open. It does not run in the background.

I do not get precise distance in tenths of a meter

Precise ranging requires ultra-wideband (UWB) support on both devices. iPhone 11 and later support it, but iPhone SE and iPhone 16e do not. If either device lacks it, you get direction and an approximate distance instead.

The arrow looks blurred

Direction cannot be determined while the phone is lying flat. Hold your iPhone upright and the arrow will sharpen.

The other person uses Android

Meettap currently supports meet-ups between iPhones only.
