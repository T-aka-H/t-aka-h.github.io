Life isn’t only made of big events. “Need milk.” “I’m tired.” “My kid seemed happier today.” Those half-formed lines are the material of an ordinary life.

Floatline isn’t an app for writing tidy tasks. You speak one line. On your device, it reads the meaning—errands become lists, promises return at the right moment, and everyday fragments grow into a journal story.

What matters isn’t storing everything. It’s being able to remember when it counts—“You mentioned this earlier.” That’s Floatline.

Processing stays on device. No ads. No account.
