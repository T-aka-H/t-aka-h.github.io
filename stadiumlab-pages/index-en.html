StadiumLab lets you design a baseball park on iPhone and iPad. Set the line and center field distances, the shape and height of the outfield fence, the shape behind home plate, stand levels and roofs, the facade, a dome, and the city or sea around the park — then look at it in 3D.

Build

  • Distances, fences, foul ground, turf and infield dirt, with a check against the official rules
  • Stand sections, levels and roofs, and a retractable dome
  • Facade materials and colors, video boards, light towers and the scenery around the park

Look around

  • From above, from the stands or from the field, by day, at dusk or at night
  • Start from any of seven sample parks

StadiumLab Pro

A one-time in-app purchase. It adds the drone tour, watching from any seat, weather, saving more than one park, detailed facilities such as hotels and bullpens, dome and stand details, game-day crowds and ambient sound, design history, and sending your park to BattingLab. It is not a subscription.

No account and no ads. Your parks are stored only on your device.
