FAQ

Does it cost anything?

The app is free. Building one park of your own and viewing it, and opening the seven sample parks, stay free. More features come with StadiumLab Pro, a one-time in-app purchase ($39.99 in the US). It is not a subscription. Prices vary by region; the App Store shows the price for yours.

What does Pro add?

  • Fly a drone over and through the park for as long as you like, with photos and video (free gives a 60-second trial each flight)
  • Watch from any seat by section, level and row (free watches from behind home plate)
  • Weather: cloud, rain, fog and snow. Time of day (day, dusk, night) is free
  • Create and save a second park and more, duplicate parks, open park files
  • Detailed facilities: hotels and other buildings, bullpens, plazas and entrances, a lit facade, video board layouts, light tower placement, foul poles, mowing patterns and more
  • Per-level stand design, seat zones and outlines; how the dome opens and its structure
  • Game-day crowds and ambient sound, design history, design sheet export
  • Send your park to BattingLab

Tap any item marked PRO in the app to see the full list and the purchase button.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap “Already bought? Restore” at the bottom of the Pro screen. It works on any iPhone or iPad signed in with the same Apple Account.

A sample says “not saved”

The free version keeps one park of your own. If you already have one, a sample opens as a preview: you can try anything, but it is not saved. To keep it, choose “Replace my current park”, or save it as a second park with Pro.

Where are my parks saved?

Only on your device. They are not synced through iCloud, so they do not move to a new device and are lost if you delete the app. To keep a park, export it as a park file (.stadiumlab) and open that file on the new device.

How do I play my park in BattingLab?

Use “Send to BattingLab” (StadiumLab Pro). Sent as a file (.blpark), it can be received and played in BattingLab without BattingLab Pro. Receiving by pasting a code is a BattingLab Pro feature. Balls do not yet bounce off roofs, lights or video boards.

Can I recreate a real ballpark?

You can combine distances, fence shapes and heights, stands, roofs, facades and scenery to get close to the park you have in mind. The app does not include data or names of real ballparks.

Are there ads?

No ads at all, and no account needed.
