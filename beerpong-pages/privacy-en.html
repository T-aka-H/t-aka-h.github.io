BeerPong: Trick & Toss — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains how BeerPong: Trick & Toss ("the app") and this website handle information.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or device identifiers. It contains no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs, and it does not send usage data to the developer's servers. Rankings use Apple's Game Center.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores on your device: your strength calibration, settings, trick-shot progress and fewest-ball records, 10-Ball Challenge records, Daily One Ball results and streaks, and match results with the cups left. Player names are not stored with match results.

To help investigate problems, the app also keeps on your device a summary of throw inputs and a log of moments when the screen slowed down. These are shared only when you choose "Send diagnostic log" in Settings, and only with the destination you pick.

3. Device permissions

The app does not use the camera, microphone, photos, location or motion sensors.

4. Replay videos

Replay videos are created on your device. They are passed only to the destination you choose in the share sheet. The temporary file is deleted after sharing.

5. Game Center and network

If you sign in to Game Center, the app uses your display name, score and rank. Your 10-Ball Challenge result (the number of cups made) is sent to Game Center, and the in-app ranking shows Game Center display names, scores and ranks. Signing in is optional; every mode can be played without it.

Game Center information is handled under Apple's services and privacy policy. You can review your profile visibility in Game Center settings on your device. Game Center & Privacy (Apple) / Apple Privacy Policy

6. In-app purchases

Payments for in-app purchases (BeerPong Pro, Expansion Pack, Expansion Pack 2) are processed by Apple's App Store. The app never receives your name, payment method or card number. Whether an item has been purchased is checked on your device with Apple's StoreKit.

7. Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the records stored on your device. "Offload App" may keep the data. Scores already sent to Game Center are managed separately by Apple.

8. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, anything you write, and your message. We use it only to reply and to investigate problems.

9. Children's privacy

The app has no chat, no photo posting and no ads. Parents can restrict Game Center features and in-app purchases with Apple's Screen Time.

10. This website

This site is served by GitHub Pages. It has no analytics or advertising cookies of its own. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security; see GitHub Pages data collection.

11. Changes

If features or data handling change, we will update this page and the date above.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy: contact us by email. Help is also on the support page.
