FAQ

How do I throw?

Touch the lower half of the screen and the ball appears under your finger. Flick toward where you aim. The flick speed sets the strength; the gauge on the right shows your last throw. Switch between Arc and Bounce at the bottom.

The strength feels off

Before your first 10-Ball Challenge you calibrate with three throws. You can recalibrate any time in Settings.

I can't make a trick shot

After 3 misses a hint shows where to hit first; after 6, the whole path. The hint also shows where to put your thumb and which way to flick. After 9 misses you can skip. Use "Look" to view the layout from any angle.

How do replays work?

Tap "Replay" after a throw. Drag to orbit, pinch to zoom and long-press to move the view. Scrub the timeline to any moment and play at ½ or ¼ speed. With BeerPong Pro you can share the replay as a video.

I'm not on the ranking

The ranking is for the 10-Ball Challenge and needs Game Center (sign in from the ranking screen). You can play without signing in.

What does it cost?

The app is free. There are three one-time in-app purchases: BeerPong Pro (CPU Normal & Hard, trick shots 16–30, replay video sharing), Expansion Pack (trick shots 31–60) and Expansion Pack 2 (61–90). Prices are shown on the purchase screen. There are no subscriptions.

How do I restore purchases?

Tap "Restore Purchase" on the purchase screen. Items bought with the same Apple Account come back.

Is there any drinking?

No. The cups are empty and there is no drinking or drunkenness. It's a skill game of tossing a ping-pong ball into cups.

How do I report a problem?

Use "Send diagnostic log" in Settings to save the log to a file and attach it to an email. Your device model, iOS version and what you were doing help a lot.
