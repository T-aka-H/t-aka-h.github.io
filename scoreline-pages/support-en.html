FAQ

How do I keep score?

Start a game from "Start Scoring" on the Home tab, then record plays on the Scoring tab. Speak plays like "single to left," or tap and flick on the field to record batted balls. Each entry appears on a confirmation card where you can confirm or adjust it.

Voice input isn't recognized well

Speech recognition runs entirely on your device. In noisy ballparks, hold the microphone closer or use tap input. Registering your lineup before the game improves recognition of player names.

I recorded a play by mistake

Tap "Undo" right after an entry to cancel it. You can also go back and correct earlier plays from the play history. Corrections are kept as part of the record, and the score is recalculated automatically.

I closed the app mid-game

Every play is saved automatically. Reopen the app and tap "Resume Scoring" on the Home tab to pick up right where you left off.

I can't generate an AI recap

AI recaps require an Apple Intelligence-capable device. On other devices, all scoring features — scores, box scores, and more — remain fully available.

Does it cost anything?

Statsline is free to use, with no ads.
