FAQ

How do I throw?

Touch the lower half of the screen and the ball appears under your finger. Flick toward where you aim. The flick speed sets the strength; the gauge on the right shows your last throw. Switch between Arc and Bounce at the bottom.

The strength feels off

Use "Calibrate strength" in Settings to recalibrate with three throws.

I can't solve a contraption level

Under the goal, the level's steps are listed (for example Plate › Door › Cup) and checked off as the ball gets there. After a miss, the button next to "Replay" starts the replay just before the step that failed. On levels with parts that move on their own, like gates and the windmill, "Same throw, now" throws the same ball again with only the timing changed. After 3 misses a hint shows where to hit first; after 6, the whole path. After 9 misses you can skip.

How do replays work?

Tap "Replay" after a throw. Drag to orbit, pinch to zoom and long-press to move the view. Scrub the timeline to any moment and play at ½ or ¼ speed. Saving a replay as a video is free three times; with Flickshot Pro there is no limit.

What does it cost?

The app is free, with chapters 1–3. There are four one-time in-app purchases: Flickshot Pro (chapters 4–6 and unlimited replay videos), Expansion Pack (chapters 7–9), Expansion Pack 2 (chapters 10–11) and Expansion Pack 3 (chapter 12). The first level of every chapter is free. Prices are shown on the purchase screen. There are no subscriptions.

How do I restore purchases?

Tap "Restore purchases" on the purchase screen. Items bought with the same Apple Account come back.

How do I report a problem?

Use "Send diagnostics" in Settings to save the log to a file and attach it to an email. Your device model, iOS version and what you were doing help a lot.
