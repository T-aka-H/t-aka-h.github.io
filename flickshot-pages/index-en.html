Flickshot: Ball Contraptions

One-ball physics puzzles

It looks impossible until you find the order. Flick a ping-pong ball and set plates, doors, swinging gates and a windmill off one after another, until the ball reaches the cup.

01

One ball, one step at a time

Bounce on the plate and the door rises, so the same ball can roll through. The steps are listed and ticked off as you go.

02

12 chapters, 131 levels

Each chapter teaches a way of throwing, then asks for it on contraptions. Chapters 1–3 are free.

03

Moving contraptions

Plates and doors, swinging gates, a paper seesaw, a windmill, a springboard, a lift, a lever, dominoes, bridges — all moved by physics.

04

Replay from where it failed

After a miss, replay from just before the step you didn't clear. Where parts move on their own, throw the same ball again with only the timing changed.

05

Replay from any angle

Orbit, zoom and slow it to ¼ speed. Three video exports are free.

06

Daily One Ball

One layout a day, three balls. Keep your streak going.
