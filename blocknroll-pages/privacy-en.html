Block'n'Roll — Privacy Policy

Home Support Privacy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

This policy explains how information is handled in Block'n'Roll (the app).

1. What is collected

The app collects and stores no personally identifying information. There is no account to create, and no analytics or advertising SDK is included.

The following is kept on your device only:

  • Which courses you have finished, and their records — time taken, number of tries, pieces used
  • Courses you were given, and the codes of courses opened recently
  • Settings (sound, haptics)

2. Processing on the device

Building courses, rolling the ball and keeping records all happen on your device. A course rebuilds itself identically from a short code, so no course data has to be stored or sent anywhere.

3. Permissions

The app requires no permissions at all — no camera, microphone, location, photos or contacts.

4. Network

The app does not use the network except in these cases:

  • Game Center — if you use the leaderboards, the app communicates with Apple's Game Center (section 5).
  • In-app purchase — buying or restoring communicates with Apple's App Store (section 6).

Giving a course away means sharing seven characters. How you share them — mail, messages — is your device's own feature and does not pass through any server of ours.

5. Game Center

Leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. If you are signed in, your league total — the number of pieces used across the set — is submitted to Game Center and shown on the board with your display name. This is Apple's service and Apple's privacy policy applies. If you are not signed in, nothing is submitted and your records stay on the device.

6. In-app purchase (Block'n'Roll Pro)

Block'n'Roll Pro is a one-time purchase that opens the Pro League, the Workshop, and unlimited drills. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit, under Apple's privacy policy. No payment details reach the developer — only the fact that a purchase exists.

7. Children's privacy

The app is rated 4+. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

8. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with the date it was last updated.

9. Contact

Questions and bug reports are welcome here.
