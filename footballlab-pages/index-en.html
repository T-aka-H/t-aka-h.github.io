Football Lab is a quarterback passing-accuracy game for iPhone. Press and hold to set, pull back to aim, release to throw. Every pass reports velocity, spin, and air yards, and the ball flies through a 3D stadium on a realistic trajectory.

Events

  • SKILLS SHOWDOWN — play all five events back to back for one total score.
  • PRECISION PASSING — thread fixed and moving rings to rack up points.
  • STRIKE OUT — knock out nine panels in as few throws as you can. Hit a seam for a double.
  • BUCKET TOSS — drop the ball into buckets at different distances, with bonus buckets by the sidelines.
  • CAN ALLEY — topple pyramids of cans; the physics makes them fall in chains.
  • LEAD THE RECEIVER — read the route and lead a running receiver. Hit the chest for a PERFECT CATCH.
  • 2-MINUTE DRILL — 2:00 on the clock from your own 25. Drive for the touchdown.

Review

Any completed throw can be replayed from multiple angles — drone, sideline, QB eyes, and the target's point of view. The PASS CHART keeps every throw from every event, and the hit rate by zone shows where you are strong and where you are not.

No account, no ads. Your records are stored only on your device.
