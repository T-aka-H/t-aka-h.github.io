Frequently asked questions

How do I throw?

Press and hold anywhere on the screen and the QB drops back. Pull your finger back and the yellow marker moves — that is where you are aiming. Release to throw. Release straight for a liner; flick upward as you release for a high bomb.

What are the bars on the edges of the screen?

They let you set direction, trajectory, and distance separately, like a real quarterback. Slide along the left edge to set the trajectory (LINER / TOUCH / BOMB) and move sideways to set the direction. Press and hold the right edge and pull down — the longer the pull, the farther the throw — then release to throw. The lever along the bottom also sets the direction. The edges only take over when you start touching from an edge; touching the middle of the screen works as before.

Can I use two thumbs?

Yes. Move your left thumb sideways to turn your eyes (direction) and use your right thumb to set and throw. You can keep turning while you are set.

How does the 2-MINUTE DRILL work?

You have 2:00 on the clock from your own 25-yard line. Pick one of three routes each play; a completion moves the ball to where the receiver caught it. Catch it in bounds and the clock keeps running — even while you pick the next play. An incompletion, or a catch on a sideline route, stops the clock. Gain 10 yards in four downs for a first down. When the clock is running you can SPIKE the ball to stop it (it costs a down).

What is the PASS CHART?

A top-down chart of every throw from every event, with the hit rate for each of nine zones (left / middle / right × short / mid / deep). Filter to your last 25, 50, or 100 throws and see at a glance where you are strong and where you are not.

How do I watch a replay?

After a throw hits its target, tap REPLAY. The throw plays back in slow motion from the target's point of view, then QB eyes, drone, sideline, and target cam. Tap the screen to end it early.

Can I change the camera?

In LOCKER ROOM on the menu, choose "Over shoulder" or "QB eyes". In QB eyes, the mini map in the top right shows where each throw landed.

How do I change the text in the end zones?

Enter a name under "Team name" in LOCKER ROOM and it is painted in both end zones.

Where is my data stored?

Your throw log, personal bests, and settings are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer's servers.
