Football Lab — Privacy Policy

Home Support

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This policy explains how Football Lab (the "app") handles information.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect or store personally identifiable information. No account is required, and it contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

The following data is stored only on your device:

  • Your throw log (trajectory, velocity, spin, hit or miss — used for the PASS CHART)
  • Personal bests for each event
  • In-app settings (throwing arm, camera, team name, control scheme, etc.)

2. On-device processing

Ball physics, hit detection, replays, and the PASS CHART statistics all run on your device. None of this data is sent to the developer's servers.

3. Device permissions

The app does not use the camera, microphone, location, photos, contacts, or any other device permission.

4. Network access

The app does not require a network connection and does not transmit data to the developer or to any third party.

5. Children's privacy

The app is rated 4+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. If a new feature changes how information is handled, the change will be described on this page.

7. Contact

For questions or bug reports, please contact us here.
