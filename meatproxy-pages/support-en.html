How to play

MeatProxy is for 3–6 players. One player taps "部屋を作る" (Host); the others in the same room tap "部屋に入る" (Join). To play with friends far away, start a FaceTime call and tap "離れた友達と遊ぶ" (SharePlay). See "遊び方・役職" in the app for the full rules.

FAQ

I can't find the room
Connect to the same Wi-Fi and turn on Bluetooth. The app needs the Local Network permission (Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network).

SharePlay won't start
During a FaceTime call, tap "離れた友達と遊ぶ" in the app. Everyone needs MeatProxy installed. To finish, tap "End SharePlay" in the app or end the call.

Can two people play?
At least three players are needed; with two, the other person's role would be obvious.

If you see offensive content

Long-press an answer or chat message to report it or block the author. The developer reviews reports and takes appropriate action. Blocked players' answers and chat are hidden on your screen, and they cannot join rooms you host. You can unblock in Settings › Blocked players.
