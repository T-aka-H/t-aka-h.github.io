MeatProxy — Privacy Policy

Home Support Privacy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains how MeatProxy ("the app") handles information.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect personally identifiable information. No account is required and there is no developer server. The app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

2. Information exchanged between players' devices

During a game, the following is exchanged only with the devices of players in the same room:

  • The display name you enter
  • Your answers to prompts, chat messages, and votes

In the same room, devices talk to each other directly (local network / Bluetooth). Over SharePlay, data reaches other players through Apple's FaceTime / SharePlay. Nothing is sent to or stored on a developer server.

3. Information stored on your device

  • Your display name, card design and timer settings, names you have blocked, prompts already played, and your agreement to the terms

All of this stays on this device only and is deleted when you delete the app.

4. On-device AI

On supported devices, the AI answer given to the Meat Proxy may be generated by Apple's on-device AI (Apple Intelligence). Processing happens on the device; prompts are not sent elsewhere. Otherwise, answers bundled with the app are used.

5. Reports

When you report an answer or chat message, your mail app opens with an email containing the reported content (prompt, text, and the author's display name). It reaches the developer only if you send it, and is used only to review and act on the report.

6. Purchases

Pro is purchased through Apple's App Store. Neither the app nor the developer receives your payment information.

7. Device permissions

  • Local Network: to find and connect to friends' devices in the same room

8. Children's privacy

The app is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us here.
