MeatProxy — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains how MeatProxy ("the app") handles information.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect personally identifiable information. No account is required. The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

The only thing that leaves your device is the anonymous usage counts described in section 2 (answers and chat are exchanged only between players' devices, as described in section 3).

2. Anonymous usage counts

To learn how much the app is played, it sends anonymous counts to collect.appline.app. Only these are counted, and only as totals:

  • How many times the app was opened
  • How many games hosted on a device were played to the end (including how many of them were played over SharePlay)
  • How many times the Pro screen was opened, the purchase button was tapped, a purchase completed, and the restore button was tapped

What is sent (and nothing else): a fixed event name (app_open / game_finished / game_finished_shareplay / paywall_shown / purchase_tapped / purchase_success / purchase_restored) and the app version (for example, 1.0).

What is never sent: display names, prompts, answers, chat, votes, scores, roles, email addresses, device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV or similar), advertising ID, location, purchase amounts or payment details, or anything else that could identify you.

You cannot be identified or tracked: the app assigns no persistent identifier to your device (no device ID, no session ID). All that arrives is how many times an action happened in total. With nothing to link those events together, it is structurally impossible to identify a particular user, follow one person over time, or build a profile. Per-person figures such as how many people use the app or how many keep using it come only from the anonymous data Apple provides in App Store Connect.

Filtered on arrival: the server records only the event names above and discards anything else it receives.

Where it goes: only to our server on Cloudflare. It is never shared with Google, Firebase, Meta or any other third party. The request passes through Cloudflare's network, but IP addresses are not recorded, stored or used, and are never linked to the counts.

3. Information exchanged between players' devices

During a game, the following is exchanged only with the devices of players in the same room:

  • The display name you enter
  • Your answers to prompts, chat messages, and votes

In the same room, devices talk to each other directly (local network / Bluetooth). Over SharePlay, data reaches other players through Apple's FaceTime / SharePlay. None of it is sent to or stored on a developer server (and none of it is part of the anonymous counts in section 2).

4. Information stored on your device

  • Your display name, card design and timer settings, names you have blocked, prompts already played, and your agreement to the terms

All of this stays on this device only and is deleted when you delete the app.

5. On-device AI

On supported devices, the AI answer given to the Meat Proxy may be generated by Apple's on-device AI (Apple Intelligence). Processing happens on the device; prompts are not sent elsewhere. Otherwise, answers bundled with the app are used.

6. Reports

When you report an answer or chat message, your mail app opens with an email containing the reported content (prompt, text, and the author's display name). It reaches the developer only if you send it, and is used only to review and act on the report.

7. Purchases

Pro is purchased through Apple's App Store. Neither the app nor the developer receives your payment information.

8. Device permissions

  • Local Network: to find and connect to friends' devices in the same room

9. Children's privacy

The app is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
