Is that line yours, or the AI's?

MeatProxy is a party game for 3–6 players. Everyone answers a prompt in a few words, but one secret "Meat Proxy" (two with 5–6 players) must submit an AI-written answer word for word. Talk it over, call out "MeatProxy!", and find who is just repeating the AI.

  • Play with devices in the same room (no internet needed)
  • Play with friends far away over FaceTime SharePlay
  • No account required
