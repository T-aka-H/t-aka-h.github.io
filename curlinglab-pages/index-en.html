Curling for iPhone & iPad

CurlingLab

Find your weight. Refine your release.

Shape each shot with weight, line, spin, and sweeping. Send a stone down a 3D arena and learn the feel of stopping it where you intend.

01 / PRACTICE

Learn your distance, one shot at a time.

Pick a target and adjust the length and speed of your swipe. Compare the distance traveled and the placement error, then repeat the same shot.

02 / RELEASE

See what your wrist twist does.

Close-up release practice connects your forward movement and wrist twist to speed, direction, and spin. Switch between diagonal and overhead views, then replay the release in slow motion.

03 / MATCH

Plan the next shot.

Play the CPU, share one device with a friend, or take turns online through Game Center. Sweep to shape the shot and follow each end, the score, and the hammer.

04 / TEN STONES

Ten shots. One accuracy score.

Compare records saved on your device. Sign in to Game Center to submit your best ten-shot score to the world leaderboard.

Follow every shot through the arena.

Move from a wide arena view to a straight view of your target, a diagonal follow shot, and an overhead angle near the house. Sliding, contact, and sweeping sounds accompany the play.

iPhone / iPad · iOS / iPadOS 17 or later

The app is available in English and Japanese, following your device language.

Practice, CPU play, and same-device matches do not require Game Center sign-in. Online matches and world rankings need internet access and Game Center. Optional motion controls require compatible device sensors.
