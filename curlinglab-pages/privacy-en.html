CurlingLab — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This policy explains how information is handled in CurlingLab and on this website.

1. Information collected

The app does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or device identifiers. It includes no advertising or tracking SDKs and no third-party analytics SDKs. Online matches and world rankings use Apple’s Game Center, as described below.

Anonymous usage counts: to improve the app, it sends only the number of times certain things happen to a developer-operated server (collect.appline.app): the app was opened; practice, a CPU match, a two-player match, or an online match was started; a Ten-Stone Challenge was started or completed; and the purchase screen was opened, the purchase button was tapped, or a purchase was completed or restored. Only three items are sent: the app name, the kind of event, and the app version. Player names, scores, delivery details, device or account identifiers, and location are never sent. Because no per-user identifier exists, events cannot be linked to a person or to each other; only totals are counted. Nothing is shared with third parties.

2. On-device storage and processing

Delivery settings, saved matches, ten-shot challenge progress and records, and pending scores or online turns are stored on your device. Records can include the player name you enter, scores, stopping positions, and summaries of delivery strength and rotation. Game Center player identifiers are used to distinguish records when accounts change.

3. Motion sensors and permissions

Optional motion controls use accelerometer and gyroscope measurements to calculate forward movement and wrist twist on your device. Raw sensor sample streams are not sent to a developer server or Game Center. You can play with swipe delivery instead. The app does not use the camera, microphone, or location.

4. Game Center and network communication

When you sign in to Game Center, the app uses player identifiers, display names, scores, and rankings. Your best ten-shot score is sent to Game Center when a challenge completes or when the app signs in or reconnects. World rankings show Game Center display names, scores, and ranks.

Online matches exchange game data, including stone positions, trajectories, scores, and turns, with the other player through Game Center. Practice, CPU play, and same-device matches do not require Game Center sign-in.

5. Information handled by Apple

Apple handles Game Center information under its services and privacy policies. Apple may process information about games you play while signed in. You can review profile and activity sharing in your device’s Game Center settings.

Game Center & Privacy / Apple Privacy Policy

Payments for the in-app purchase (CurlingLab Pro) are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app never receives your name, payment method, card number, or other payment details. It only checks on your device, through Apple’s StoreKit, whether the purchase has been made.

6. Deleting stored data

Deleting local app data removes the records saved on that device. Offloading the app may retain its data. Scores and match information already sent to Game Center are managed separately by Apple; deleting local app data does not also delete Game Center data. Consult Game Center settings and Apple’s guidance to manage information held by Apple.

7. Support correspondence

If you email support, we receive your email address, any name or other information you choose to provide, and the contents of your message. This information is used as needed to answer questions and investigate problems. Please use the same contact address for questions about, or requests to delete, support correspondence.

8. Children’s privacy

The app does not provide its own chat service, photo uploads, or advertising. Apple Account settings and device restrictions apply to Game Center use. Parents can restrict Game Center features through Apple’s Screen Time controls.

9. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added website analytics or advertising cookies. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub’s explanation of data collection on Pages.

10. Changes to this policy

When features or data practices change, this page and its last-updated date will be updated.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy, email CurlingLab support. The support page also explains how to use the app.
