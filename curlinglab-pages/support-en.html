Frequently asked questions

How do I start practicing?

Open “アリーナに行く” and choose a target with “目標”. Swipe upward on the delivery pad. After the shot, compare the distance traveled and placement error. Select “もう一投” to try the same target again.

How do delivery and sweeping work?

Swipe length and speed adjust weight; sideways motion fine-tunes the release line. While the stone travels, rub the pad from side to side and choose distance or curl control. Enable “スイープ ON” for sweeping in practice. The ten-shot challenge uses no sweeping so that it measures delivery accuracy.

What is motion release practice?

From “アリーナに行く”, open “センサーのリリース練習”. A close-up stone shows how your grip, forward movement, and wrist twist affect speed, direction, and spin. Choose a diagonal or overhead view and replay the release in slow motion. This practice does not add shots or scores to matches or the ten-shot challenge.

How should I hold the device for a motion delivery?

Face the top edge of the screen forward, with the screen angled slightly upward. Hold the pad, move the device forward, twist your wrist, and lift only the finger touching the screen. Keep holding the device. Sideways tilt alone does not create spin. Choose “投球へ” to return to normal practice with motion delivery selected.

What if motion sensors are unavailable?

Motion delivery needs compatible accelerometer and gyroscope sensors. Swipe delivery remains available. The example releases in the close-up practice screen can be viewed without sensors.

How do local records and world rankings differ?

Ten-shot records are saved on your device. When signed in to Game Center, your best score is submitted to the world leaderboard and shown with your Game Center display name and rank. If a submission cannot be sent, it is kept pending for a later attempt, such as after reconnecting. You can compare local records without using world rankings.

What do I need for online play?

You need internet access and Game Center sign-in. Two players use separate Game Center accounts and start through a friend invitation or automatching. Matches are turn-based: finish your delivery and send the result to pass the turn. They do not stream the other player’s controls in real time.

What if Game Center cannot connect?

Check your internet connection and Game Center sign-in in device settings. For an unsent online turn, reconnect and use the retry action shown in the app. If no opponent is available, try a friend invitation, CPU play, or a two-player match on one device.

How can I delete my records?

Deleting local app data removes the records saved on that device. Offloading the app may retain its data. Scores submitted to Game Center are managed separately and are not removed simply by deleting the app. See the privacy policy for details.

Which devices and languages are supported?

CurlingLab supports iPhone and iPad with iOS / iPadOS 17 or later. The in-app interface is currently Japanese. Optional motion controls require compatible device sensors.
